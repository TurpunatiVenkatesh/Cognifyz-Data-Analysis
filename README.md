## Overview

An end-to-end exploratory analysis of the Zomato restaurant dataset, covering cuisine demand, city performance, pricing, delivery impact, ratings, geography and restaurant chains.

## Level 1: Key Insights

| Analysis | Key Finding |
|---|---|
| **Top Cuisines** | North Indian dominates with **41.46%** of restaurants, followed by Chinese (28.64%) and Fast Food (20.79%) |
| **City Analysis** | New Delhi leads with **5,473** restaurants; London is the top-rated city at **4.54** (cities with 20+ restaurants) |
| **Price Range** | Budget segment holds **46.53%** of the market; premium segment only 6.14% |
| **Online Delivery** | Only **25.66%** offer delivery, yet they average **3.25** vs 2.47 without it |

## Level 2: Key Insights

| Analysis | Key Finding |
|---|---|
| **Restaurant Ratings** | Most common rating range is **3-4** (4,388 restaurants); average votes per restaurant: **156.91** |
| **Cuisine Combinations** | North Indian alone is most common (936); Cafe has the highest average rating (**2.89**) among the top 10 combinations |
| **Geographic Analysis** | **83.4%** of mapped restaurants (7,549 of 9,052) are in the Delhi NCR region |
| **Restaurant Chains** | 112 chains with 5+ outlets; Barbeque Nation leads in rating (**4.35**) with only 26 outlets |

## Business Takeaways

- Affordable, North Indian and Chinese cuisines capture the largest share of demand.
- Delhi NCR is highly saturated with lower average ratings, signalling a quality gap.
- Delivery-enabled restaurants rate higher, showing a clear expansion opportunity.
- More outlets do not guarantee better ratings: smaller chains can outperform large ones.
